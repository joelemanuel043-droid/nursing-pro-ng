💊 Drug Guide

Learn medicines safely

Study a medicine by checking its generic name, class, indication, contraindications, adverse effects, interactions, monitoring needs, and patient education.

This prototype does not provide patient-specific prescribing or dosing. Confirm medicines and doses using an approved formulary, prescription, and clinical supervisor.
🏥 Clinical Hub

Clinical learning checklist

  • Hand hygiene and appropriate PPE.
  • Confirm patient identity and explain care respectfully.
  • Assess and record vital signs accurately.
  • Maintain privacy, dignity, and infection prevention.
  • Document observations and promptly report deterioration.
  • Perform procedures only after training, authorization, and supervision.
